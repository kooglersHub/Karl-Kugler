## Looking for a quiet dinosaur shirt

I was looking for a subtle dinosaur shirt for adults. That led to a small motif on the front and a larger illustration on the back. I developed designs around ferns, moths, axolotls and other subjects I wanted to explore.

## Letting people look closely

I built the web preview so visitors can compare designs, cuts and colours and read the story behind each motif. Direct links make it possible to share a specific combination. The page needs to help people understand the print before they choose a garment.

## Before selling

The website is a public design and shop preview with no active sales. Garment mock-ups are labelled as digital concepts. The image here is the digital print artwork. Samples, print checks and fit would come before a shop launch.
