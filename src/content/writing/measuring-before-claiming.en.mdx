A hand model can seem perfectly aligned with a sensor. A digital creature can look like it is intentionally searching for food. Both pictures invite an explanation before the evidence is ready.

## Separate the states

In [HaptIQ Hand](/en/projects/haptiq-hand/), a possible placement, a promising fit and a confirmed identity are different results. Approximate geometry and uncalibrated taxel positions limit what a convincing fit can establish. In [EvoLab](/en/projects/evolab/), one surviving population does not establish that inherited weights caused that outcome.

## Leave room for a different explanation

A frozen snapshot makes a placement estimate easier to reproduce. An appropriate control group helps me question an apparent improvement. A control still needs care: disabling reception while keeping the cost of sending tests a different thing from removing a channel and all its costs.

## Put the uncertainty next to the result

I want a provisional placement to be labelled next to the candidate. I want missing ancestry to stay visible as missing in an organism inspector. Clear interfaces can make the distinction between observation, interpretation and confirmation easier to see.
